Lesson 5 · 17 min

Query with impressions and no page: validated topic

A paid tool estimates what people search for. Your Search Console shows what they already searched for and where you appeared. A non-branded query with impressions and no page made for it is a validated topic, for free. In this lesson you sort topics, pages to improve and noise, and you write the brief with a real asset.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1What an owner page for a query is
  2. 2Topic, improve, or ignore
  3. 3Why each topic needs a real asset
  4. 4The brief for a new page

Goal: turn non-branded queries with impressions and no owner page into a topic list of 5 pages, each backed by a real asset.

01 · From query to topic

Click each block.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Two pages for the same search compete with each other. Before writing, ask whether the owner page already exists.

02 · From list to brief

Click each step.

1. Filter

Keep the non-branded queries that have impressions and make sense for your business.

A search that has nothing to do with you (a typo, a stray name) is noise: ignore it.

2. Sort

For each query, look for the owner page on your site.

No owner: topic. Owner outside the top: improve the owner page.

At INEMA (week of 10/05 to 10/11/2026)The non-branded queries that appeared the most were in Spanish: "aprender ia" (10 impressions), "aprender inteligencia artificial desde cero" (10), "cómo aprender ia" (8). All landed on the Spanish page about learning AI, between positions 29 and 61. They already had an owner page: the case is to improve, not to write another one.

3. The real asset

Every new page rests on a real asset: a course, a project, an experiment, data from your business. Without an asset, the topic waits.

That is what keeps the page from being just another copy of the rest.

4. The gate

A person approves the list before anything is written. At most 5 pages at a time.

No 5 variations of the same keyword: that is content at scale, and Google treats it as spam.

04 · Practice: five topics with an asset

Use the most recent week of your export.

E1 · The ranked list

40 min

Take the 15 non-branded queries with the most impressions and sort each one into topic, improve, or ignore.

Done when: the list has 15 rows with impressions, best position, owner page (or "none"), and decision.

E2 · One brief

30 min

Pick the strongest topic that has a real asset and fill in the brief.

Done when: the brief has a query, intent, a named own data point (a course, a project, or a number of yours), and 2 older pages that will link to the new one.

06 · Take with you

Owner page firstIf the owner page exists, improve it. If it doesn't, it goes on the topic list.
Real assetWithout a course, project, or data of your own, the topic waits.
2026 yardstickGoogle's guide for AI (May/2026) asks for non-commodity content: your own data and direct experience. It's the same filter as the real asset.
