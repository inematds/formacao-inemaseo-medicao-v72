Lesson 8 · 20 min

Weekly and monthly cadence and the Plan B for days 30 to 60

SEO pays off in weeks and months, not days. What makes the result is the routine: one look per week, one battery of checks per month and, between days 30 and 60, an answer for each symptom instead of giving up. And one precaution almost nobody takes: Search Console keeps only about 16 months. If you don't archive, you lose your own history.

02 · Routine, Plan B, and archive

Click each step.

1. Weekly

Pick a fixed day and time. Run the export (or the spreadsheet), inspect the key URLs, and write down one decision.

One decision per week is enough: change a title, write a topic, strengthen a page.

At INEMAThe weekly report runs every Tuesday morning and only generates files; a person decides and publishes.

2. Monthly

Run the same 20 to 30 questions in the same assistants, in an incognito window, and note whether your site appears, with which address, and who appears in its place.

A paid "share of voice" tool is a sample, not an official score. A local model does not measure what the real AIs answer.

3. Plan B

Read the symptom before acting: few indexed, indexed with no impressions, impressions with no clicks, position 11 to 30 stalled, a drop after publishing a lot.

Each one has a response in the table below.

4. Archive

Save the resumo.csv and the non-branded files from every week in a folder with a backup.

Next step: the gsc-historico tool imports that folder into a local database that never deletes anything and generates the up, down, and topic report.

03 · The Plan B table, the schedule, and the archive

Replace what is between < > with your own. The gsc-historico commands come from the tool's README; the details of the tool are covered in course 7.

Plan B (inemaSEO method)
| symptom (day 30–60) | likely cause | what to do |
|---|---|---|
| few pages indexed | crawling, wrong canonical, thin page | URL Inspection, internal links, noindex on the thin ones, request indexing again |
| indexed, almost no impressions | keyword with no demand or lack of authority | revalidate with Search Console data; change the topic; backlinks |
| impressions with no clicks (CTR < 0.5%) | weak title/description | title with a specific promise and a number; measure for 2 weeks |
| position 11–30 stalled | content ok, low authority | external links to the winners; new sections for the queries that showed up |
| overall drop after publishing a lot | signal of content at scale | stop, audit with the checklist, merge/remove the weak ones, lower cadence |
Schedule (template)
Every <Tuesday> <9am>: non-branded export + inspection of the key URLs + 1 decision (30 min)
Every day <1>: battery of <25> questions in <4> assistants (1 h)
Day <30> and day <60> after publishing: read the symptom and apply Plan B
Every <Friday>: check that the week went into the archive folder

    05 · Practice: the routine on your calendar
