Lesson 7 · 18 min

Conversion goal and AI-sourced traffic in GA4

Search Console shows who arrived from search. It doesn't say what the person did afterward, or who came from an AI assistant. That's what analytics is for: a conversion goal with the source page and a channel of its own for visits coming from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and the like. Some of them land in "Direct"; the lesson shows why.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why a visit is not a result
  2. 2A conversion goal with the source page
  3. 3An "AI Assistants" channel in GA4
  4. 4Why some AI traffic shows up as Direct

Goal: create a conversion goal with the source page and a channel of its own for visits coming from AI assistants in GA4.

01 · From visit to conversion, by source

Click each block.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

The AI number in GA4 is a floor: what has a marked source. The rest is hidden in Direct.

02 · Goal and channel in four steps

Menu names change over time; look for the name of the function. Click each step.

1. The goal

Pick an action that shows real interest: joining the community, asking for a quote, downloading the material.

One well-chosen goal is worth more than ten events nobody looks at.

At INEMAThe method includes the "Join the community" event with the source page. In INEMA's analytics (Plausible), this goal was still listed as pending in October 2026. The goal is the part that most often gets pushed back; put it on your calendar.

2. The key event

Fire an event when the action happens, and mark that event as a key event in the GA4 admin.

Check in the Realtime report that it shows up when you click it yourself.

3. The AI channel

In the admin, look for Channel groups and create a custom group based on the default.

Add the "AI Assistants" channel with the source rule from the regex below, and place it above Referral; the order decides which rule catches the visit.

4. The reading

Cross channel and landing page with the key event. Ask: which page brings people who convert?

Don't compare your number with studies: ChatGPT's share of AI traffic ranges from 63% to 92% depending on the sample.

Made-up numbers to teach the reading. Few AI visits, but a high conversion ratio: it's worth looking at which pages they opened.

  • Floor, not total: a visit with no source lands in Direct.
  • Perplexity: according to a blog report, GA4 doesn't recognize it on its own; that's why it's in the regex.
  • Studies disagree: ChatGPT's share ranges from 63% to 92% depending on the sample; don't use it as a goal.

04 · Practice: goal and channel connected

No GA4? The same applies to Plausible or another analytics tool: goal + source.

E1 · The key event

40 min

Pick the action that matters, fire the event with the source page, and mark it as a key event.

Done when: you clicked the button and the event showed up in the Realtime report, with the source page.

E2 · The AI channel

30 min

Create the custom channel group with the "AI Assistants" channel above Referral.

Done when: the group shows up in the acquisition reports; if there was already a visit from chatgpt.com or perplexity.ai, it is in the new channel, not in Referral.
